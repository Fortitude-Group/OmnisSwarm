The text in rasterised frames (the GIF export) is drawn from Spleen 8x16 2.2.0 by Frédéric Cambus, released under the BSD 2-Clause licence (https://github.com/fcambus/spleen/releases/tag/2.2.0); the licence text is in `LICENSE-spleen.txt`. `spleen8x16.ts` is generated from the release's `spleen-8x16.bdf` by `scripts/build-font.ts`. `extra.ts` holds our own drawings of the few glyphs Spleen lacks.
